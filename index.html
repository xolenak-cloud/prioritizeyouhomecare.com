Our Story

Built From Loss.
Driven By Love.

My name is Lena Maxfield — wife, mama of five, Vice President of the PTA at Myer Elementary, and a woman deeply rooted in this community.

After losing my mother and my sister to cancer — back to back — I made a decision. I wasn't going to just grieve. I was going to DO something.

That's how Prioritize YOU Caregivers was born. Not just to serve seniors, but to support anyone navigating illness, recovery, or the daily weight of caregiving — because I know firsthand what it feels like when a family is going through it without the right support. We show up because someone should have shown up for mine.
